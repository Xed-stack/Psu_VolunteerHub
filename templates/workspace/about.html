About PSU Volunteer Hub

PSU Volunteer Hub connects Pangasinan State University campuses, coordinators, and volunteers through campus activities and documented service achievements.

How the System Works

  1. Volunteers

    Register, choose a campus and interests, discover activities, review the agreement, and join. Follow commitments in your calendar.

  2. Coordinators

    Upload an approved proposal, review details, publish an activity, record attendance during the event, and generate campus reports.

  3. Director

    Review university and campus delivery, verify annual goals, manage accounts and policies, and export reports.
