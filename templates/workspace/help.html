Saved drafts and account help

Signed-in forms show their save status. Wait for “Draft saved” before leaving. Resume or discard unfinished work from Saved drafts. Passwords and unsubmitted agreement acceptance are never saved.

For access or account problems, contact your campus Extension Coordinator. A deactivated account can request reactivation from the sign-in screen.
