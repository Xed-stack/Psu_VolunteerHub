Help guide

How to use the Volunteer Hub

Simple answers for volunteers and external participants.

Create your volunteer profile

Select your campus, interests, and skills during registration. These choices help the system recommend relevant opportunities.

Create an account

Find and join an activity

Browse by campus or category, open the activity details, then select Join Now. Sign in with your PSU account or use the external-volunteer option.

Browse opportunities

Cancel a registration

Open your volunteer dashboard. Eligible future registrations include a cancellation action. Completed or attended activities cannot be cancelled.

Attendance and participation

Your campus coordinator records attendance after the activity. Completed participation then appears in your dashboard history.

How recommendations work

Recommendations compare your interests and skills with activity requirements using cosine similarity. They support your decision and do not guarantee suitability or a slot.

Account problems

For a deactivated account, submit a reactivation request from the sign-in screen. For other access issues, contact your campus Extension Coordinator.
